## Hypotheses and separate gates

**Exposure:** each new case must actually dispatch the complete source header
with strong proof and a known clear call, followed by the source V0 LICH at its
actual source position. Prefix cases must reach the accepted V0 body with a
real incomplete/empty sample interval before its end. The corrupted case must
actually expose V0's parity rejection. Missing exposure fails this gate; zero
voice calls alone cannot pass an unexercised active-boundary claim.

**Finite voice safety:** every real synthesis call must belong to a fully
delivered 36-dibit voice slot. Use per-slot real body calls, not the current
vocoder cursor or a frame-wide EOF flag: all body reads precede voice processing.
A fully received early slot remains eligible after EOF is discovered in a later
slot. Partial/empty slots have unavailable source positions and may not be
credited from returned dibits, plausible bits, nonzero PCM or successful status.
Record any actual unavailable-slot synthesis as a quality failure, without
suppressing it, padding EOF, resetting the vocoder or substituting silence.

**Complete-slot retention and routing:** every actually fully received source
voice slot in a prefix must route exactly once with the correct source bits at
both real FEC return and synthesis input. Partial slots are not required to
produce speech. Match source/frame/slot identity before bit comparison. Preserve
the strict source-phase bound `-20 < deviation < 20`; reject ambiguous mapping,
duplicate/stolen occurrences and unconfirmed routes. An all-frame abort that
discards a fully received early slot fails this retention gate.

**Parity and subsequent clean retention:** no voice, FEC or synthesis belongs to
the parity-rejected V0. The other 20 transmitted voice occurrences must retain
their source identity and exact bits; the header's known clear epoch must remain
correct through source V1..V4 and both half-steal survivors, ending at the real
trailer with TERMINATOR. Do not reinterpret lack of bad-frame exposure as a
successful rejection or successful recovery. Later clean losses remain failures.
Sticky confirmation may survive the rejection: return value 1 is not itself
acceptance of that frame. Require actual parity rejection, no current proof or
current evidence, and no target voice calls; do not impose return value zero.

**Observation integrity:** preserve the prior finite body-call and optional
CRC/LICH event contract. Require common receiver, FEC, synthesis-input and logical
call-state equality across detail modes and chunks. Do not compare random PCM
values or seed the vocoder. Preserve real negative returns and named flags;
never inspect uninitialized predecode fields. The common wrappers remain active
in both modes; no uninstrumented-app equivalence is claimed.

For header RAN publication, retain actual processing order: SACCH precedes
strong FACCH, so initial live RAN may remain -1 until the next eligible valid
SACCH. Call state before FEC may mark media active without certifying speech.
Prefix EOF need not behave like a received trailer; observe cleanup separately.
